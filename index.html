02 Edge AI · Computer vision

Livestock Calving Detection System

Real-time edge vision that watches livestock video streams and alerts farm operators when calving begins.

  • Multi-class YOLOv8 behaviour model detecting pre- and post-calving events: posture (sitting angle ≥90° sustained >10s), tail raise (>15s), amniotic sac appearance and calf emergence.
  • Full inference pipeline deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Orin, with frame deduplication and stream-level optimisations to sustain real-time throughput at the edge.
  • Targeted data augmentation for robustness under variable farm lighting and camera angles; automated real-time alerts enable timely veterinary intervention.

  • TensorRT
  • OpenCV
